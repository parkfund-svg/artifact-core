ABOUT QUANTREA-X

What is Quantrea-X

An AI autotrading program that combines an AI that designs strategies with a local engine that executes them quickly and consistently. No coding required — describe your trading idea in plain language and it becomes an executable strategy.

💡 How Quantrea-X Works

1. Design (AI)

The AI analyzes your trading idea described in natural language and assembles it into a strategy graph made of nodes (conditions, indicators, orders).

2. Verify (Backtest)

The assembled strategy is immediately backtested against historical prices, and the AI reviews the results (win rate, Sharpe ratio, MDD) to refine the strategy.

3. Execute (Local Engine)

Once verified, the strategy graph runs repeatedly on the local engine without further AI calls — fast, low-cost, and running continuously even with no screen open.
