DEVELOPMENT HISTORY

Development History

Our recent progress.

Early Jul 2026

Built a Stable Risk Management Foundation

Precisely calculated option margin and fees by market, and reflected real-time valuation including open positions. Also added a feature that automatically records the moments before and after each trade.

Mid Jul 2026

Completed the Strategy Builder's "AI Auto-Improvement Loop"

Completed a flow where a strategy is backtested the moment it's created, with the AI reviewing the results and suggesting improvements. Premium nodes like candle anatomy and higher-timeframe analysis also began being added around this time.

Late Jul 2026

Completed Unattended Multi-Account Autotrading & Phase-Switching Strategies

Built infrastructure to run multiple accounts unattended at the same time, so trading keeps working even after closing the screen. For the three strategies that automatically switch by market phase (bullish/bearish/sideways), live-style testing confirmed a simulated backtest return of 152% (Sharpe ratio 2.8) for the bull-phase strategy — this is a simulated backtest result, not real-account performance. The Android app also launched around this time.

Aug 2026

AI Bot Signal Control & Predictive-Candle Rule Trading

Added a setting to follow AI bot signals as-is, reverse them, or auto-switch based on market phase, along with a new "predictive-candle rule trading" mode that automatically enters and exits based on predictive candles. Later expanded the predictive-candle trading system into a triple-AI verification structure with Gemini, Claude, and GPT, so trades only reflect predictions when all three AIs agree on direction.

Aug 2026

Brand Renewal — Refreshed as Quantrea-X

Refreshed the company and product names, and renewed the brand with a new full-video homepage.

Aug 2026

Comprehensive Chart UI Overhaul

Refined the readability and feel of the chart screen across the board.

Aug 2026

Mobile App Background Execution Support

Improved the Android app so that checking trading status and running autotrading continue uninterrupted even while the app is in the background.
