FEATURES

Key Features of Quantrea-X

Every feature works on its own, or combined with the others.

Strategy Builder

Node-Based Strategy Builder

Describe your trading idea in chat and the AI assembles it into condition, indicator, and order nodes. The strategy is backtested immediately, and you can refine it through conversation based on the results — like noticing "losses were large because there was no stop-loss."

Trade Lines

Chart Trade Lines

Draw entry, take-profit, and stop-loss lines on the chart, and orders fire automatically when conditions are met. Tag multiple entries — like scaling in — and connect each to a different take-profit line.

Predictive Candle

AI Predictive Candles

AI predicts the next candle's movement and displays it on the chart. The 5-candle statistical ensemble runs 5 times and takes a majority vote for higher reliability, while the single-candle prediction forecasts both the current candle's close and the next candle — and can even auto-execute as "predictive-candle rule trading."

Triple Verification

AI Triple-Verified Predictive Trading

Gemini, Claude, and GPT each independently predict the next candle, and a trade is only placed when all three agree on direction. Instead of relying on a single AI, different AIs cross-verify one another.

AI Bot

AI Bot Autotrading

Choose your preferred engine — Gemini, Claude, or GPT — to analyze the order book and price action and execute trading decisions in real time. You can follow the signal as-is, reverse it, or have it switch automatically depending on market phase.

Phase Detection

Phase-Adaptive Autotrading

Indicators for trend strength and volatility determine whether the market is trending up, down, or sideways. Only the strategy suited to the current phase runs automatically, preventing forced trades in the wrong conditions.

Unattended

Unattended Multi-Account Operation

Strategies, trade lines, and AI bots for multiple accounts keep running in the background even without the screen open. View running strategies and P&L for every account in one place.

Mobile

Mobile Support

Check trading status and run autotrading from the Android app, not just on PC.
